# Verification

Verification checks receipt evidence and its digital commitments. Read the result and individual checks together: authentic historical evidence is not a guarantee that its reviewed content is correct.

## Match local files

When file matching is available, select the local copies you want to check. Their contents stay on the device. Matching confirms that their digital fingerprints agree with the receipt.

## Current and original evidence

Original evidence preserves the issued record. A current receipt can include later lifecycle information. Withdrawal, supersession or expiry may affect current eligibility without changing the original review statement.

## Offline checks

The standalone offline verifier makes no network requests. It checks the supplied bundle and status snapshot, but cannot discover withdrawals or other changes published after that snapshot. Obtain a fresh receipt or current status check when you need current information.
